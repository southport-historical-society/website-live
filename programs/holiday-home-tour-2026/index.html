2026 Holiday Tour of Homes

December 11 & 12, 2026

Celebrate the Holidays, Southport Style!

One of Southport’s favorite holiday traditions returns this December! The Southport Historical Society invites you to enjoy beautifully decorated homes and the distinctive charm, character, and history of our coastal community.

This year, there are two ways to enjoy the tour: a new guided walking experience on Friday and our traditional self-guided tour on Saturday.

Tour Details & Tickets

Choose Your Tour

Friday, December 11 — NEW! Guided Walking Tour

$75 per ticket

1:30–5:00 p.m.  |  200 tickets total

Visit nine beautifully decorated Southport homes as part of a guided group following a planned route and scheduled visits.

Please note the physical demands: this experience includes approximately two miles of walking and 3½ hours of walking or standing, with limited opportunities to sit or rest. Please consider your physical abilities before purchasing.

No advance ticket pickup is needed. Your purchase confirmation receipt will provide the private check-in location and instructions. At check-in, each Friday guest will receive a tour ticket and one complimentary raffle ticket for the holiday wreath featured on the cover of the 2026 Holiday Home Tour Keepsake Booklet.

Saturday, December 12 — Traditional Self-Guided Tour

$35 per ticket

11:00 a.m.–5:00 p.m.  |  1,000 tickets total

Enjoy our traditional Holiday Tour of Homes at your own pace. Visit nine decorated homes and a historic church, choosing your own route and schedule during tour hours. Discover the homes’ architecture, stories, and festive touches as you explore Southport.

Saturday tickets must be picked up by the purchaser before beginning the tour. Pickup details appear below.

A Sneak Peek at This Year’s Homes

From traditional Southport cottages to contemporary residences, this year’s tour celebrates the variety of homes that make our community special.

We are grateful to the homeowners and church community who generously open their doors for this holiday tradition.

Ticket Sales

Members-Only Presale

Members of the Southport Historical Society have an opportunity to purchase tickets before they go on sale to the general public.

Eligibility: Your membership must have been current as of September 30, 2026.

Each eligible membership may purchase up to TWO TICKETS TOTAL, for EITHER Friday OR Saturday — not both. This limit is per membership, regardless of whether it is an individual or family membership.

TourReserved for presaleTicket price
Friday guided tour100 tickets$75
Saturday self-guided tour500 tickets$35

Presale tickets are first-come, first-served and are not guaranteed. Eligible members will receive an email with the presale dates, purchase link, and instructions. Presale purchases are subject to verification against membership records. The purchase link is for eligible members only and should not be shared.

Need more than two tickets? Members may also shop during the public sale, subject to availability.

General Public Ticket Sales

Thursday, November 19, 2026, beginning at 10:00 a.m.

Any presale tickets not purchased by members will be carried into the public sale. The number of public-sale tickets therefore depends on presale demand:

TourTickets available at public openingTicket price

Saturday self-guided tour500–1,000 tickets$35

For perspective, 701 Saturday tour tickets were sold during last year’s public sale.

Public-sale purchases are limited to FOUR TICKETS PER TRANSACTION. This is not a four-ticket lifetime or per-person limit. Public sales are open to everyone, including members who want additional tickets or tickets for the other tour. All tickets are sold online, subject to availability.

Tickets and Check-In

Friday Guided Walking Tour

No advance ticket pickup required. Guests receive their tour tickets at event check-in, along with one complimentary raffle ticket for the wreath on the cover of the 2026 Holiday Home Tour Keepsake Booklet. The private check-in location and instructions will be supplied in the purchase confirmation receipt.

Saturday Self-Guided Tour

The purchaser must pick up Saturday tour tickets at the Fort Johnston Southport Museum and Visitor Center.

Pickup dates: Saturday, December 5, through Saturday, December 12, 2026.
Hours: Monday–Saturday, 10:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m. Closed Sunday, December 6.

Tickets are not mailed. Pick up your Saturday tickets before starting the tour. On tour day, pickup closes at 4:00 p.m., even though the tour continues until 5:00 p.m.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I attend both Friday and Saturday?
Yes, subject to ticket availability. During the members-only presale, you may buy up to two tickets for one tour only. Additional tickets for either tour may be purchased during the public sale.
Can I buy four tickets during the public sale?
Yes. Public-sale purchases are limited to four tickets per transaction, including transactions by Society members.
Does a family membership receive more presale tickets?
No. Every eligible membership has the same maximum of two presale tickets in total.
Can I join now to qualify for the 2026 members-only presale?
No. Eligibility is based on memberships that were current as of September 30, 2026. New members can enjoy future membership benefits.
Am I guaranteed presale tickets if I am eligible?
No. A limited number of tickets are available first-come, first-served. Eligible purchases are checked against Society membership records; ineligible or above-limit purchases will not be honored.
I qualify but did not receive a presale email. What should I do?
Email info@southporthistoricalsociety.org so we can review your eligibility and contact information.
Do Friday guests need to collect tickets at Fort Johnston?
No. Friday guests receive their tickets at event check-in. Their confirmation receipt will include the private check-in details.
What if I accidentally purchase more presale tickets than allowed?
Contact info@southporthistoricalsociety.org promptly. Purchases outside the eligibility or ticket-limit rules cannot be honored.
Will the tour go ahead if it rains?
Yes. The Holiday Tour of Homes takes place rain or shine.

Not a Member Yet?

New memberships purchased after September 30 do not qualify for the 2026 presale, but membership offers opportunities to enjoy benefits in future years.
